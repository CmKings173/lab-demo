# ADR 001: Product scope is AI Server and AI Workstation

## Context
The advisor must recommend infrastructure for local AI workloads without becoming a general IT catalog.

## Decision
Support only AI Server and AI Workstation.

## Reason
This keeps contracts, filters and demo behavior aligned with the business use case.

## Alternatives considered
Support all ITShop categories or laptops.

## Consequences
The catalog and workflow can reject out-of-scope product types explicitly.
