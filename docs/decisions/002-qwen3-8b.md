# ADR 002: Qwen3-8B is the Lab 1 baseline

## Context
Lab 1 needs one behavior model before hardware benchmarking.

## Decision
Use Qwen3-8B as the named baseline; keep the exact repository variant open.

## Reason
It is the user-provided target and keeps the training contract concrete.

## Alternatives considered
Start with a larger model or a hosted model.

## Consequences
Training and serving remain local and must be benchmarked before production claims.
